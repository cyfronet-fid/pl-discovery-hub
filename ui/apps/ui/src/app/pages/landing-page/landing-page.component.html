Our Data Sources

Our data sources bring together trusted research outputs, institutional repositories, and verified scientific datasets in one connected ecosystem. Access reliable, high-quality data from across Poland and Europe ready to discover, reuse, and build upon.

ROHub
ROHub enables the management, sharing and preservation of research artefacts related to a particular investigation/research challenge as a single information unit through the Research Object (RO) concept and model.
Poznan Supercomputing and Networking Center
RODBUK
Cracow Open Research Data Repository
The purpose of RODBUK is to collect, develop, archive and make available in open access all types of research data created by researchers, PhD candidates and students in the course of scientific activity.
Academic Computer Centre CYFRONET AGH
Most Wiedzy Open Research Data Catalog
The nature of the ‘Bridge of Data’ project is to design and build a platform that allows collecting, searching, analyzing and sharing open research data and to provide it with unique data collected from the three most important Pomeranian universities
Gdańsk University of Technology
RepOD Repository for Open Data
RepOD is the first Polish repository for open research data. All researchers, regardless of affiliation and academic discipline, can create a free account and start sharing data produced, collected, or described during research activities.

Related Initiatives

EOSC Beyond
EOSC Beyond is a project developed within the European Open Science Cloud framework to strengthen collaboration among researchers across Europe. It introduces new capabilities to the EOSC Core services and helps research service providers access, integrate, and share resources more effectively.
eosc.gov.pl
EOSC.gov.pl is an initiative of the Polish EOSC partnership that supports the development of open science in Poland. It provides information on national EOSC-related activities and serves as a space for sharing experiences and good practices among institutions involved in open science.
EOSC Association
EOSC Association brings together institutions and organisations committed to building an open scientific ecosystem in Europe. It supports collaboration, common standards, and the development of sustainable infrastructure that improves access to research data, tools, and services.

About eosc.pl

The Polish Open Science Platform is part of the European Open Science Cloud, which supports open access to research resources across Europe. As a national EOSC node, the platform helps researchers access data, publications, and infrastructure for advanced research and international collaboration.
